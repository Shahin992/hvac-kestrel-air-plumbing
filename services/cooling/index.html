AC repair

When your air conditioner needs help

Most summer breakdowns come down to a handful of parts. Our trucks carry them, so the fix usually happens on the first visit.

Warm air from vents

A failed capacitor, contactor or low refrigerant charge.

Ice on the lines

Restricted airflow or a refrigerant leak. Turn the system off and call us.

Water around the unit

A clogged condensate line or a full drain pan, often a quick fix.

Runs nonstop

Dirty coils, the wrong size system or a failing compressor.

Breaker keeps tripping

An electrical fault that needs a technician. Don't keep resetting it.

Higher summer bills

Efficiency drops as systems age or lose refrigerant charge.

AC repair starts with a $79 diagnostic visit, waived when you approve the repair. Common repairs such as a capacitor or contactor run $180 to $420.

Cooling FAQ

Air conditioning questions

How long should a central air conditioner last?

Most central air conditioners last 12 to 17 years. Systems that get a yearly tune-up and clean filters tend to reach the upper end of that range.

What does SEER2 mean?

SEER2 is the current federal efficiency rating for air conditioners and heat pumps. A higher number means the system uses less electricity to deliver the same cooling.

Why is my AC running but not cooling?

Check that the thermostat is on cool and the filter is clean. If the outdoor unit isn't running, or the indoor lines are icing, turn the system off and call us. Running it can damage the compressor.
