Financing FAQ

Financing questions

Will checking my financing options affect my credit score?

No. Pre-qualifying uses a soft credit check, which does not affect your score. A full credit check only happens if you choose a plan and accept the offer.

Can I pay off my loan early?

Yes. There are no prepayment penalties on any of the plans we offer.

Can I finance a repair, or only new systems?

Financing is available for any project over $1,000, including larger repairs, water heaters and repiping.
