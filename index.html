How much does a furnace or AC repair cost in Columbus?

Our diagnostic visit is $79 and is waived when you approve the repair. Most common repairs, such as a capacitor, igniter or flame sensor, cost between $150 and $450 in parts and labor. You always see the flat price before any work begins.

Do you offer same-day and emergency service?

Yes. Calls booked before 2 pm are usually seen the same day, and our emergency line is answered 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Kestrel Club members never pay after-hours or weekend fees.

How often should my furnace and air conditioner be serviced?

Once a year each: the furnace in the fall and the air conditioner in the spring. A yearly tune-up catches worn parts early, keeps the manufacturer warranty valid and helps the system run efficiently.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Columbus and the surrounding towns, including Westerville, Dublin, Hilliard, Worthington, Upper Arlington, Gahanna, New Albany, Powell, Grove City, Reynoldsburg and Delaware.

Are your technicians licensed and insured?

Yes. Kestrel holds Ohio HVAC and plumbing contractor licenses and carries full liability and workers' compensation insurance. Our HVAC technicians are NATE-certified and EPA 608 certified, and every employee is background-checked.

Do you offer financing on new systems?

Yes. Qualifying systems over $3,000 can be financed at 0% APR for 24 months, or with fixed-rate terms of up to 120 months. Pre-qualifying takes a few minutes and won't affect your credit score.
